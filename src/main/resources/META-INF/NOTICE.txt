OSRS Wiki content
The bundled Slayer catalogue adapts text and strategy information contributed
by the Old School RuneScape Wiki community:
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/

Except where a source specifies otherwise, post-1 October 2018 article content
is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 3.0
Unported. Adapted Wiki text in this distribution retains that license; the
plugin's BSD license does not replace it. The full CC license is included in
LICENSE-CC-BY-NC-SA-3.0.txt in this directory:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/

Changes include extracting, combining, summarizing and restructuring Wiki
material into task, monster, location, equipment, route and preparation data.
Source article URLs and revision evidence are retained in the bundled data
under /data/ and in the source repository's src/main/data/slayer directory.
The plugin's Guide panel links to its sources. Article history pages identify
the contributing authors. Earlier revisions and separately licensed material
retain their applicable terms. See the Wiki's additional licensing terms:
https://meta.weirdgloop.org/w/Licensing
