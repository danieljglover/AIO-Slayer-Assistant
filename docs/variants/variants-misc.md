## Gaps
- Spiritual creatures: Zamorak variants (ranger/warrior/mage) have all-zero defensive bonuses, so MELEE vs MAGIC
  is a tie on bonuses; resolved to MAGIC via the Air elemental weakness. Melee is equally effective.
- TzHaar (Hur/Mej/Xil/Ket/Jad): all melee defence bonuses are 0; weakness resolved to MAGIC via Water elemental
  weakness, but melee works equally well on raw bonuses.
- King kurask: infobox does not list `immunecannon`; regular kurask is cannon-immune so King kurask is assumed
  immune but not explicitly confirmed on its page.
- Kurask "Big" (id 411) and Waterfiend's two ids share identical published stats (no per-id stat split on wiki).
- Warped Terrorbird and Warped Tortoise: best-melee is crush, but magic (magic def 0) is also fully viable;
  Terrorbird has no elemental weakness per wiki.
