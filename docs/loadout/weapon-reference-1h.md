## Notes on method & scope
- **Every row** of the one-handed slot table is included, including poison variants `(unp)/(p)/(p+)/(p++)`, charged/uncharged, normal, in/outside-ToA, and Deadman/blighted variants exactly as the wiki lists them.
- Offensive columns captured verbatim from the slot table: Stab/Slash/Crush/Magic/Ranged **attack** bonuses, **Strength**, **Ranged strength**, **Magic damage %**, **Prayer**. (Defensive columns intentionally omitted — out of scope.)
- **Attack speed** is the slot table's per-item Speed column in **ticks** (× 0.6 = seconds). Spot-checked against known values (whip 4, d scim 4, fang 5, rapier 4, zaryte cbow 6) — all consistent.
- **Style / attack type** is derived from the dominant offensive bonus + weapon class (melee stab/slash/crush, ranged, magic). It is the *intended/dominant* style; full bonuses are shown so you can see secondary styles.
- **Special attack** column = `Name (energy%): effect`, joined from the Special attacks page by base weapon name (applies to all variants of that weapon).
- **Passive** notes are a curated subset for notable weapons (vs-category bonuses, accuracy rerolls, degradation, autocast-only). Absence of a note ≠ no passive for obscure items.
